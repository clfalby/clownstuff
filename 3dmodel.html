Meet Barry Bear

Homie had known his friend Barry since they were both adopted to the circus as toddlers. Homie wanted Barry to come with him to Turks and Caicos but Barry was afraid to leave home. Barry thought he might be profiled by the customs officials as a violent offender and unable to travel. Barry tried to convince Homie to stay and work things out with Vera but to no avail.
