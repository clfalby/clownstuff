Homie the Clown's Holiday

Homie was tiring of all the noise and hustle of the state fair. He needed to get away from Vera, his ex-girlfriend who was hassling him to go back to college. Homie dreamed of Sun, Surf and Mai Tais under an umbrella. He booked a trip to Turks and Caicos.
