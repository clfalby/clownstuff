The Festival of Despair

Life at the carnival was becoming banal for Homie. He spent his days doing the same routine over and over for his audience. The circus tent was old and drab. The rides dangerous and needing a fresh coat of paint. His shoes were caked in mud. There was a sadness that hung over the midway like a dark cloud.
